Clinica Push 4: Silent Infarction Reveal and Temporary Prologue Loadout

Goal:
After the warning scene, reveal the Silent Infarction trap and show a temporary story loadout before the prologue battle.

Silent Infarction reveal:
Visible enemies should look weak and easy at first.
Then:
- Red heartbeat pulse crosses battlefield
- Injured civilians deteriorate
- Monitoring crystals show conflicting information
- Safe tiles become unstable
- Dark energy travels beneath the player
- Fog reveals new silhouettes
- Escape path becomes blocked

Dialogue:
Nightingale:
“Their condition is deteriorating!”

Fleming:
“The visible creatures are not the source!”

Master Bai:
“Fall back! Now!”

Silent Infarction:
“The strongest healers are often the easiest to deceive.”
“They see what they expect to see.”
“And act before they understand.”

Freeze the battle as the trap closes.

Temporary loadout:
Show a prologue loadout screen over the blurred or darkened isometric battlefield. Keep the Former Self trapped in the distance with fog and red pulse effects.

Required temporary units:
1. The Former Self
2. Florence Nightingale, temporary legendary support hero
3. Sir Alexander Fleming, temporary legendary assessment/debuff hero

Rules:
- Nightingale and Fleming are required.
- Nightingale and Fleming cannot be removed.
- These temporary legendary units must not enter the permanent roster.
- This is a story loadout, not the permanent player loadout.

Former Self strengths:
- Powerful direct attacks
- Strong healing
- High stats
- Spectacular skills

Former Self weaknesses:
- Limited assessment
- Poor reassessment
- Weak team synergy
- Resource-heavy abilities
- Bonuses encourage acting alone
- Reduced ability to identify concealed threats

Nightingale role:
- Observation
- Environmental assessment
- Hidden-hazard detection
- Stabilization
- Reassessment

Fleming role:
- Enemy analysis
- Resistance identification
- Targeted intervention
- Preventing adaptation
- Treatment correction

Loadout dialogue:
When Nightingale is added:
“I will watch for the dangers hidden between the obvious ones.”

When Fleming is added:
“And I will ensure that our response is guided by evidence rather than assumption.”

After both:
Master Bai:
“A capable team does not exist to admire your strength.”
“It exists to challenge your judgment.”

Confirm button:
Enter the Battlefield

Subtext:
Assess. Prioritize. Intervene. Reassess.

Acceptance checks:
- Silent Infarction trap feels hidden and intelligent.
- Loadout preserves battlefield context.
- Nightingale and Fleming are required and cannot be removed.
- Temporary units do not transfer into permanent roster.
- Confirm button enters prologue battle.
