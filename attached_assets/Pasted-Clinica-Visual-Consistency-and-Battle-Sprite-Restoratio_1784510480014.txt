Clinica Visual Consistency and Battle Sprite Restoration Plan

Goal:
Restore the original visual identity for Clinica’s heroes and fix the current issue where battle sprites are not showing. The original 10 heroes from about one day ago should become the master reference for all hero art going forward.

Core art rule:
Each hero must have two paired visual forms:

1. Battle Sprite
- Used only inside battles.
- Style: 2.5D hand-drawn tactical RPG sprite.
- Reference style: the original 10 Clinica hero sprites from about one day ago.
- Similar feel to Eiyuden Chronicle or Suikoden-style hand-drawn RPG sprites.
- Small but expressive.
- Isometric or 2.5D angle.
- Readable silhouette.
- Slight chibi/tactical proportions, but not childish.
- Clear action pose.
- Grounded shadow.
- Medical-fantasy outfit details simplified enough to read at battle size.

2. Full-Body Portrait
- Used on main pages, hero profile, recruitment reveal, dialogue, story popups, class screens, and hero detail screens.
- Style: full-body donghua / fantasy anime / Genshin Impact-inspired character illustration.
- Transparent background.
- Elegant, polished, heroic, readable on mobile.
- Same character identity as the battle sprite.
- More detailed clothing, face, hair, accessories, and fantasy-medical design than the sprite.

Important:
The full-body portrait and the battle sprite must look like the same character. They should share:
- Gender presentation
- Face/hair identity
- Main color palette
- Outfit theme
- Class/family role
- Medical-fantasy tool or weapon
- Silhouette
- Personality

Do not mix unrelated portrait art and sprite art for the same hero.

Major current bug:
Sprites are not showing in battles.

Fix this globally:
- Audit the battle renderer.
- Audit hero visual data.
- Audit all hero IDs and visual IDs.
- Audit sprite asset loading.
- Audit sprite fallback logic.
- Audit z-index/layering.
- Audit opacity/visibility.
- Audit battlefield positioning.
- Audit scale and anchor points.
- Audit whether heroes are rendering offscreen.
- Audit whether sprite paths or keys were changed during today’s art update.
- Audit whether the new prologue heroes bypass the original sprite pipeline.

The fix should not only patch Florence Nightingale and Alexander Fleming. It must fix the entire roster.
