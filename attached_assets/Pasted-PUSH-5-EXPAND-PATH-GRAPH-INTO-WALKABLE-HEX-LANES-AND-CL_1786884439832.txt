PUSH 5 — EXPAND PATH GRAPH INTO WALKABLE HEX LANES AND CLEARINGS

Convert the approved logical route graph into the exact Chapter tile footprint.

Target tile counts:

Ch 1–5:
60

Ch 6–10:
70

etc.

==================================================
1. TILE BUDGET FORMULA
==================================================

Do not spend the entire tile budget on pathways.

Use approximate allocation:

55–65%
LANES

25–35%
CLEARINGS

5–15%
TRANSITIONS / CONNECTORS

These are generation targets, not rigid percentages.

Example 60-tile Chapter:

~34 lane cells
~18 clearing cells
~8 transition / junction cells

The final exact unique tile count must equal the Chapter target.

==================================================
2. CLEARING COUNT FORMULA
==================================================

Suggested baseline:

clearingCount =
  clamp(
    round(tileCount / 10),
    5,
    12
  );

Therefore approximately:

60 tiles:
6 clearings

70:
7

80:
8

90:
9

etc.

Topology may modify by ±1–2.

==================================================
3. CLEARING TYPES
==================================================

Not every clearing has a predetermined encounter.

Clearings are spatial opportunities.

Generate types such as:

GENERAL_CLEARING
JUNCTION_CLEARING
SIDE_CLEARING
MAJOR_CLEARING
FINAL_CLEARING

Actual encounter contents remain randomized per run.

==================================================
4. CLEARING SIZES
==================================================

Small:
3–5 cells

Normal:
5–8 cells

Major:
8–12 cells

Boss/landmark:
8–14 cells where the topology permits.

Do not make every clearing perfectly circular.

Possible shapes:

- oval
- crescent
- widened intersection
- rectangular-ish training court
- offset plaza
- irregular open bay

==================================================
5. PATH TO CLEARING TRANSITIONS
==================================================

A lane should visibly widen into a clearing.

Avoid:

tiny corridor
→ abrupt giant room

Prefer:

2-cell lane
→ 3-cell transition
→ open clearing

This will also make the generated raster map look architecturally plausible.

==================================================
6. CLEARINGS SHOULD HAVE MULTIPLE EXITS WHEN APPROPRIATE
==================================================

Central clearings:

usually 2–4 exits.

Side reward clearing:

may have one entry.

Major junction clearing:

3–5 connections.

This produces natural navigation.

==================================================
7. SPATIAL VARIATION
==================================================

Do not place every clearing evenly apart.

Use:

- one larger central zone
- several smaller areas
- occasional clustered clearings
- longer connector between some zones

depending on MapDNA.

==================================================
8. EXACT TILE COUNT
==================================================

After lane widening and clearing expansion:

if tileCount < target:
expand eligible open regions or widen selected lanes.

If tileCount > target:
prune low-value perimeter cells while preserving connectivity.

Do NOT truncate randomly.

==================================================
9. FINAL CONNECTIVITY
==================================================

BFS from start.

Require:

reachableCells === targetTileCount

No disconnected hex islands.

Commit:

feat(journey): expand route graph into lane and clearing hex footprint
