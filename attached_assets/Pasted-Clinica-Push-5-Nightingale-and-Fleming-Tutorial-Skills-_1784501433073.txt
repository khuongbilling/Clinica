Clinica Push 5: Nightingale and Fleming Tutorial Skills

Goal:
Add the first prologue battle teaching mechanics through Nightingale and Fleming.

Return to the frozen battlefield after confirming the prologue loadout.

First tutorial action:
Nightingale enters or steps forward.

Nightingale:
“Do not attack yet. Give me a moment to assess the field.”

Prompt the player to use:
Lamp of Observation

Effect:
- Reveals hidden hazards
- Highlights deteriorating civilians
- Identifies unsafe tiles
- Shows visible enemies are decoys or secondary threats
- Reveals patient status indicators
- Thins fog in selected areas

Visual effect:
- Warm lamp glow expanding across tiles
- Hidden symbols briefly appearing
- Hazard tiles outlined
- Patient status indicators becoming visible
- Fog thinning

Second tutorial action:
Fleming enters or steps forward.

Fleming:
“Now examine the enemy before selecting an intervention.”

Prompt the player to use:
Culture and Sensitivity

Effect:
- Reveals one enemy weakness
- Reveals one resistance
- Marks ineffective attacks
- Shows repeated untargeted attacks contribute to adaptation

Visual effect:
- Green-blue analytical lines around enemy
- Sample or scan animation
- Weakness and resistance icons
- Brief tactical information panel

Master Bai lesson:
“What you discover should change what you do next.”

Then allow the player to select the next action.

Rules:
- Keep tutorial text short and mobile-readable.
- Highlight only one mechanic at a time.
- Do not overload the screen.
- These skills belong to temporary prologue Nightingale and Fleming, not permanent roster versions.
- This push should teach assessment before intervention.

Acceptance checks:
- Lamp of Observation works in the prologue battle.
- Culture and Sensitivity works in the prologue battle.
- Player learns hidden hazards and targeted intervention.
- Master Bai’s lesson appears after both mechanics.
- Prologue flow continues safely to playable battle actions.
