Clinica Push 10: Memory Echo Rewards and University Introduction

Goal:
Reward the opening prologue without giving full legendary Nightingale and Fleming as permanent heroes.

Nightingale and Fleming do not remain in the permanent roster.
They survive as instructional Memory Echoes.

Award automatically after post-rebirth awakening:
1. Nightingale’s Lamp Fragment
2. Fleming’s Culture Notes

Nightingale’s Lamp Fragment:
Type: Support Memory Echo
Beginning effect:
Reveals one concealed clue, environmental hazard, or hidden symptom.
Flavor text:
“A fragment of warm lamplight remains within your reconstructed memory. It illuminates what haste once caused you to overlook.”

Fleming’s Culture Notes:
Type: Assessment Memory Echo
Beginning effect:
Reveals one enemy weakness, resistance, or ineffective treatment category.
Flavor text:
“Faded notes from a forgotten battlefield remain legible. They remind you that the strongest treatment is not always the correct one.”

Echo scene:
A faint image of Nightingale appears.

Nightingale Echo:
“Observe what has changed before deciding what must be done.”

Her image fades and leaves the Lamp Fragment.

A faint image of Fleming appears.

Fleming Echo:
“Study the cause. Then choose the intervention.”

His image fades and leaves the Culture Notes.

System message:
“Two instructional memories survived Lotus Recall.”
“These are not heroes.”
“They are lessons you were not yet prepared to accept.”

Memory Echo visual rules:
- Translucent illustrated silhouettes
- Partially formed portraits made from memory fragments
- Warm gold light for Nightingale
- Cool blue-green analytical light for Fleming
- Brief appearances only
- Echo cards form from light
- Cards should look like preserved memory artifacts, not full hero cards

Gameplay rules:
- Both Echoes are automatically awarded.
- Both begin automatically equipped.
- Echoes use separate tutorial slots.
- Echoes do not consume normal hero positions.
- Each may initially be used once per battle.
- Effects stay simple during early onboarding.
- They cannot be upgraded immediately.
- Later progression depends on learning and related quests, not only player level.

Future full hero locks:
- Full legendary Nightingale remains locked for a later environmental care, monitoring, or patient-safety story quest.
- Full legendary Fleming remains locked for a later infection-control and antimicrobial-resistance story quest.
- Do not unlock either full legendary hero immediately after rebirth.

University introduction:
After echo rewards, introduce Clinica University as the first destination.

Suggested copy:
“You were not recalled because you were ready.”
“You were recalled because you can still learn.”
“Begin with Lotus Lessons.”

Highlight:
- Lotus Lessons
- Simulations
- Research Library
- Class Tree or future growth
- Learn, then apply in Ward Shift

Acceptance checks:
- Echo rewards are awarded once only.
- Echoes do not occupy hero slots.
- Nightingale and Fleming are not permanent full heroes.
- Full legendary versions remain locked.
- University is clearly introduced as the next destination.
- No duplicate rewards occur on replay or reload.
