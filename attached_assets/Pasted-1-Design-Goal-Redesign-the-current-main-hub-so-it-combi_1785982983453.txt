1. Design Goal

Redesign the current main hub so it combines:

The functionality and complete feature coverage of the current implementation.
The premium coloration, typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy of the generated reference.
A clear separation between:
permanent game navigation;
Sanctuary hub shortcuts;
contextual tutorial and progression information.

The final result should feel like a polished fantasy-medical anime RPG, not a standard web dashboard placed over fantasy artwork.

The central hero and the Enter the Ward button must be the two strongest visual elements.

19. Journey Map Integration

Journey should become the permanent destination for all map and chapter systems.

Include:

Scrollable chapter map
Fog-of-war tile mode
Route selection mode
Chapter progression
Boss-key collection
Chapter bosses
Area restoration or alternate chapter gimmicks
Fog-of-war tile rules
Movement costs stamina
Moving onto a tile reveals adjacent or selected fog
Tiles may contain:
battle encounter;
treasure chest;
traveling merchant;
area boss.

Area bosses grant keys or key fragments required to unlock the chapter boss.

Traveling merchant rates may vary by chapter and should remain data-driven rather than hard-coded into the UI.

The Journey tab should remain highlighted on all chapter-map variants.
