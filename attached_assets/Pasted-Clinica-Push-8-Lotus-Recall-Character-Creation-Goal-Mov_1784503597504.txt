Clinica Push 8: Lotus Recall Character Creation

Goal:
Move permanent character creation after the scripted defeat and make it feel like identity reconstruction inside Lotus Recall.

Intro text:
“Your strength has been taken from you.”

“Your title has been forgotten.”

“Your achievements belong to a life that has already ended.”

“Yet something remains.”

“A will to heal.”

“A desire to understand.”

“And one final opportunity to begin again.”

Display:
Who will you become this time?

Visual environment:
- Shallow reflective water
- Floating lotus petals
- Broken fragments of Former Self armor
- Luminous medical symbols
- Faint silhouettes of past companions
- A large reflection or mirror made from light
- Dark background with teal, violet, gold, and white illumination
- Slow camera movement
- Ripples with each selection

Character reconstruction animation:
Start with featureless humanoid silhouette.
As player makes selections:
1. Former Self mask dissolves
2. Legendary armor breaks into particles
3. Body shape changes
4. Facial features appear
5. Hair and skin options materialize
6. Beginner outfit replaces legendary gear
7. Starting tool or clinical focus forms from broken fragments
8. High-level aura fades
9. Smaller beginner aura remains
10. New character opens eyes after confirmation

Character creation options:
- Name
- Pronouns
- Appearance
- Body and face options
- Hair
- Skin tone
- Starting outfit
- Starting weapon, tool, or clinical focus
- Personality-oriented questions
- Initial learner or healthcare pathway recommendation

Rules:
- This creates the permanent Level 1 identity.
- The Former Self is gone, but potential remains.
- Questionnaire may recommend a class/pathway but should not permanently lock the player.
- Player can choose another option.
- Preserve choices if player closes the app mid-creation.

Acceptance checks:
- Character creation happens only after defeat.
- It feels connected to Lotus Recall, not a generic form.
- Permanent player identity is created after confirmation.
- Choices persist through reload.
- Returning players are not forced into creation again.
