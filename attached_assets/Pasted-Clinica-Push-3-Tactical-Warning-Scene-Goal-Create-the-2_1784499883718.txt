Clinica Push 3: Tactical Warning Scene

Goal:
Create the 2.5D tactical battlefield warning scene where Master Bai, Florence Nightingale, and Sir Alexander Fleming warn the Former Self not to rush.

Map style:
Use a 2.5D isometric tactical presentation, not flat top-down.
Include:
- Grid-based terrain
- Elevation differences
- Stairs, ledges, ramps, and platforms
- Damaged clinical sanctuary or emergency treatment plaza
- Cracked walkways
- Elevated treatment platforms
- Abandoned stretchers
- Medical carts
- Broken lanterns
- Glowing monitoring crystals
- Torn banners
- Triage tents
- Injured NPCs
- Scattered clinical records
- Fog-covered areas
- Red energy moving under the ground
- Weak visible decoy enemies
- Distant, partially hidden Silent Infarction silhouette

Color direction:
- Cool blue-gray moonlight for the environment
- Warm gold for Nightingale
- Green-blue analytical effects for Fleming
- Jade-white for Master Bai
- Deep red-black subtle pulse effects for Silent Infarction

Characters:
- Former Self / The Prodigy
- Master Bai
- Florence Nightingale
- Sir Alexander Fleming
- Silent Infarction

Dialogue staging:
Characters should move and react while speaking. Use steps, turns, kneeling, raising a lamp, examining samples, pointing toward fog, drawing weapons, and reacting to red pulses. Use camera pans and zooms where possible.

Required dialogue:

Master Bai:
“Stop where you are.”

The Prodigy:
“We have already wasted enough time.”

Master Bai:
“Time spent observing is not time wasted.”
“Something is wrong. The enemy is allowing us to see only what it wants us to see.”

The Prodigy:
“You taught me to trust my training.”

Master Bai:
“I taught you to question your first conclusion.”
“Confidence without reflection is merely another form of blindness.”

Nightingale:
“These injuries do not match the enemies in front of us.”

The Prodigy:
“Then we will ask the enemy after we defeat it.”

Nightingale:
“The environment is part of the assessment.”
“Their breathing changed before they collapsed. The monitors failed before the first creature appeared. We are missing something important.”

The Prodigy:
“Or you are searching for complexity where none exists.”

Nightingale:
“A patient does not become less endangered because the danger is difficult to see.”

Fleming:
“This corruption is adapting.”

The Prodigy:
“Everything adapts shortly before I destroy it.”

Fleming:
“That certainty is precisely what concerns me.”
“The organisms at the edge are already resistant to our first response. If we attack carelessly, we may strengthen what we are trying to eliminate.”

The Prodigy:
“Then I will strike hard enough that resistance will not matter.”

Fleming:
“Power used without proper selection can turn treatment into harm.”

Final warning:
Master Bai:
“Wait for the others. Assess the field. Form a plan.”

The Prodigy:
“While we stand here discussing possibilities, people are suffering.”
“I have defeated stronger enemies than this alone.”

Master Bai:
“That is why this enemy has chosen you.”

The Prodigy:
“Watch carefully, Master.”
“I will show you how quickly this can be ended.”

End:
The Former Self advances alone and the Silent Infarction begins its trap reveal.

Acceptance checks:
- Tactical battlefield feels isometric and deep.
- Dialogue is readable on mobile.
- Characters visually react during dialogue.
- Mentors give distinct warnings.
- Former Self sounds heroic but impatient, not cruel or stupid.
- Scene ends with Silent Infarction trap setup.
