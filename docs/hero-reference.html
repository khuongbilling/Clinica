Clinica · Hero Reference

Complete roster of all 104 launch heroes organized by family and tier. Includes Foundation Banner pull rates, image asset availability, and in-game usage. Read-only reference — no game content was changed.

Tier System & Pull Rates

All heroes belong to one of four tiers. These rates govern the type of hero summoned per pull. Individual hero rates depend on the specific banner pool.

1★
65%
Common
Base-pool heroes; high availability. Evolves into Uncommon.

Uncommon

3★

Rare

4★

Epic
Top-tier heroes with unique Leader Specialties and evolved forms.

In-Game Usage — All Modes

Ward Shift — Main battle. Deploy 3-hero team; use skills, AP, clinical cues, items, and care chains.
Mission Loadout — Choose your 3-hero team before a Ward Shift. Slot 1 = Leader, grants bonus skill.
Ward Defense — Tower defense. Deploy hero units on the ward board to intercept Corruption enemies.
Realm — Assign heroes to Realm buildings for +25% production per assigned hero.

All heroes also participate in: Recruitment (gacha) · Training Hall (XP + levelling) · Hero Evolution (dupe copies → star upgrade) · Hero Audit (balancer tools).

Foundation Banner — Pullable Heroes (18 slots)

The Foundation Banner is the primary gacha pool. It contains a curated set of 18 hero slots (including 2 ghost/future slots). Total weight pool: 274. Cost: 100 Codex Shards per single pull. Duplicate = 25 Codex Shards refund.
